🌸 KNOWLEDGE FOR CHANGE

Beti Bachao Beti Padhao

Save the Girl Child • Educate the Girl Child

🇮🇳

Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP) is a flagship national initiative launched by the Government of India in January 2015. The scheme addresses the critical issue of declining Child Sex Ratio (CSR) and aims to empower women and girls across the country.

🎯

Core Objectives

  • 🛡️ Prevent gender-biased sex selection and ensure the survival and protection of the girl child.
  • 📚 Ensure education and participation of the girl child in society.
  • 💜 Challenge and change deep-rooted mindsets and patriarchal stereotypes regarding daughters.
🌍

Key Strategy: A Two-Pronged Approach

📣

Mass Media Campaign: A nationwide awareness and advocacy campaign to shift public mindsets and celebrate the birth and education of girls.

🤝

Multi-Sectoral Action: Targeted, measurable interventions in districts with a critically low child sex ratio, through cooperation between Women & Child Development, Health & Family Welfare, and Education ministries.

✨ Every daughter deserves safety, education, opportunity, and respect. ✨
